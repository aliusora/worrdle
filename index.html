How to play

Guess today's word in six tries.

The word has five letters. It is a term used in recruiting and keeping research participants (i.e., recruitment and retention, or R&R). It is always either a jargon term or its plain-language counterpart.

Each guess must be a five-letter word in the game's word list. After you submit a guess, each tile shows how the letter fits:

  • S✓ Carolina blue ✓
    In the word and in the right spot.
  • T● Yellow ●
    In the word but in a different spot.
  • U– Gray –
    Not in the word.

After your fourth guess, a Hint button appears. It's optional. It tells you whether the word is a jargon term or a plain-language term.
